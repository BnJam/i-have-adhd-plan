# Research rationale

This document explains why the skill emphasizes externalized state, bounded
actions, calibrated estimates, and recovery. It is design rationale, not a
clinical guideline and not evidence that the skill diagnoses or treats ADHD.

## Evidence-to-rule map

| Finding | Skill implication | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adults with ADHD showed a large impairment in complex task planning, while plan recall, self-initiation, and execution effects were smaller in one prospective-memory study. [Fuermaier et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23484020/) | Make planning concrete, externalize the current state, and connect plans to an immediate action. | The study used a controlled task and should not be treated as a universal profile. |
| A 2023 review found adult ADHD time-perception research scarce and mixed across estimation, reproduction, and management. [Mette](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36833791/) | Use time ranges with assumptions and recalibrate after a checkpoint instead of asserting a fixed “time blindness” mechanism. | The review itself calls for more consistent research. |
| A 2024 systematic review identified psychological training, medication, digital interventions, and exercise as executive-function approaches, with only five included digital-intervention studies. [Ramos-Galarza et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39064248/) | Treat digital agent scaffolding as an evidence-informed design hypothesis, not a clinically validated intervention. | The review includes heterogeneous populations, methods, and outcomes. |
| A 2024 adult ADHD CBT trial included organization/planning, distractibility, and procrastination modules; benefits were observed at one-year follow-up. [Wang et al.](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12888-024-05673-8) | Support repeated, practical process steps rather than one large plan dump. | The intervention was therapist-led CBT alongside medication, not an AI skill. |
| A 2024 randomized study of the “Let’s Get Organized” intervention found time-management improvements that persisted at three months, but no significant difference from individual occupational therapy. [Holmqvist et al.](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.5014/ajot.2024.78S2-PO269) | Make organization and time management explicit support targets without claiming superiority or treatment effect. | The sample included ADHD, autism-spectrum, and mental-health populations. |
| A 2026 study found preliminary improvement in everyday prospective memory when adults with ADHD used episodic future thinking, but the effect was no longer significant after controlling for verbal ability. [Altgassen et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41684048/) | Offer a brief future-state cue only when useful; never require it or present it as a proven ADHD intervention. | The sample was modest and the result was preliminary. |

## Design boundaries

- The skill is an accessibility preference and can be useful without an ADHD diagnosis.
- The agent must not infer diagnosis, treatment needs, motivation, or emotional state.
- Adaptation is evidence-led: ask one context question only when the answer would change the safe plan.
- Externalized checkpoints support continuity, but they do not justify claiming that unverified work survived an interruption.
- A cause must be labeled as unknown when the available evidence does not establish it.
